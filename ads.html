Access and payment

You stay the owner. Always.

This is how we work with every ads client, so your business is never in someone else's hands.

  1. You give us access to your ad account only. On Meta this is done through Business Manager, as a partner. No passwords needed.
  2. You pay for ads directly to the platform with your own card. You see every shilling spent.
  3. You can remove our access any time. The page, the ad account and the data stay yours.
  4. We never ask for your social media passwords. If anyone asks you for them, be careful.

Questions

Questions about ads

How soon will I see results?

Ads start showing within a day of approval. The first one to two weeks are for testing, so we learn which audience and which creative bring customers. After that we put more of your budget behind what works.

How much should I spend on ads?

It depends on your goal and your market. You can start small. We recommend a daily budget that fits what you want to achieve, and you pay it directly to the platform.

Do you shoot the videos and photos?

Usually we give you the ideas, the script and the exact way to shoot, and you film with your own phone and products. Real content from your business tends to perform well. If you want us to shoot, tell us and we'll arrange it.

Can you also reply to comments and DMs?

Yes, as a custom page management package. Some businesses prefer to talk to their customers themselves, so we shape it around how you work.

My ad account is restricted. Can you help?

Yes. We find out why it was restricted and take it through the platform's review process. The final decision is the platform's, but acting quickly and correctly gives you the best chance.

Will I get reports?

Yes. You see what was spent, what came back and what we're changing next, in plain language.
