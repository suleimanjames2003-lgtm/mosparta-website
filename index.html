Questions

Questions people ask us

I want to run ads but I don't know where to start. What do I do?

Start with our free review. Send us your Facebook or Instagram page and we'll tell you what to fix before you spend any money on ads. Then you decide if you want us to run the ads for you.

Can you guarantee I'll get customers?

No honest agency can promise sales, because results also depend on your product, price and how fast you reply to customers. What we promise is the work: a proper setup, creatives that are tested, money moved to what works, and clear reports so you see where every shilling went.

I'm scared of losing money. I've never done this before.

That's normal, and it's exactly who we built Mosparta for. You can start with a small budget, you pay the platform directly so you always see what's spent, and we stop anything that isn't working.

How much do your services cost?

Every business is different, so we don't put prices on the website. Tell us what you need, we understand your situation, then we send you a quote privately on WhatsApp. Payment is discussed after we agree on the work.

Do you work with businesses outside Tanzania?

Yes. Most of our work happens online, so we serve clients outside Tanzania too.
