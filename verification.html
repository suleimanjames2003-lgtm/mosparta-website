Two routes

Paid or notable: which one fits you?

We tell you which route makes sense before you spend anything.

Paid verification (Meta Verified)

A monthly subscription from Meta for creators and businesses who confirm their identity. Faster and open to more people. The badge stays while the subscription is active.

Notable verification

For public figures and brands that are widely known and covered in the media. No subscription, but the platform reviews it strictly.

What usually helps

Get these right before you apply

Each platform sets its own requirements and they change from time to time. We check the current ones for your case.

A complete profile

Real photo or logo, a clear bio and a working link.

An active, clean account

Regular posts and no history of breaking the rules.

Matching identity

Your name on the account matches your official documents.

Public presence (for notable)

Articles about you or your brand in known media.

Who decides?

The platform makes the final decision on every badge. What we control is preparing your account and your request so it has the best possible chance.

Questions

Questions about the blue tick

How long does it take?

Paid verification is often quicker. Notable verification can take longer because the platform reviews it carefully. We tell you what to expect for your case before we start.

Will the blue tick bring me more followers?

Not by itself. It builds trust, makes it harder for others to copy you, and tells customers they found the real you. Growth still comes from content and ads.

Can the badge be removed later?

Yes. Platforms can remove it if an account breaks their rules, changes its name, or if a paid subscription stops.

I was rejected before. Can you still help?

Often, yes. We look at why it was rejected and what needs to change before trying again.
