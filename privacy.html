Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 Nov 2026

Who we are

Mosparta Digital Hub, Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. Contact: contact@mosparta.com or +255 622 050 743.

What we collect

Our forms do not save anything on our website. When you press the button, your answers open in WhatsApp on your own phone, and nothing is sent until you press send.

When you message, call or email us, we receive what you choose to share: your name, number, account links and details of your request.

Cookies and measurement

We may use the Meta Pixel and similar tools to understand which pages and ads bring visitors, and to measure clicks on WhatsApp, call and email buttons. These tools may set cookies in your browser. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

We remember your language choice in your browser so the site opens in the same language next time.

How we use your information

  • To reply to you and deliver the service you asked for
  • To send you a quote and agree on the work
  • To improve our website and ads

Sensitive cases

For account recovery and harmful content cases, we keep what you share confidential and only use it to work on your case. We don't share your story, name or screenshots with anyone outside the case.

Passwords

We never ask for your social media passwords for ads work. We work through ad account access that you give and can remove at any time.

Sharing

We don't sell your information. We share it only when needed to deliver your service, for example with a platform during a recovery or verification request, or when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by messaging +255 622 050 743 or emailing contact@mosparta.com.
