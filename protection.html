The honest truth about results

For disabled accounts and content removal, the final decision belongs to the platform. We follow their official process properly and tell you honestly where your case stands. We won't promise you 100%, and you should be careful with anyone who does.

Questions

Questions about protection

How long does recovery take?

It depends on the platform and the case. Some accounts come back in a day or two, others take longer because we wait for the platform's review. We keep you updated at each step.

Can you find or hack the person who did this?

No. We work only through the platforms' official processes. That's what gets accounts back and content removed without putting you at more risk.

Can you remove a post from someone else's account?

We report it through the platform with the right evidence. When it breaks the platform's rules, it is often removed. The final decision is the platform's.

Will anyone else know about my case?

No. Your case stays between you and the people working on it. We don't share stories, names or screenshots.
